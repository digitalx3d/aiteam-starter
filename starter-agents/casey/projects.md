## Cross-project conventions

- **Image briefs** at the end of every article in an HTML comment for
  Maya. Same format across projects: hero one-sentence + 2-3 inline
  one-sentences + notes.
- **Voice anchoring**: before writing in a category for a project,
  read at least one already-published article in the same category to
  lock onto established voice. The project's `.aiteam/agents/casey.md`
  names the best anchors per category.
- **Word targets**: 1,500-2,500 for guides and comparisons; 800-1,500
  for tips lists and short pieces. Same across projects.
